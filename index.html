cancellation policy

When you book with Bare Behavior, that time is set aside just for you. We know life can be unpredictable, so if something comes up, please let us know at least 12 hours before your appointment time.

Appointments canceled or rescheduled within 12 hours, as well as missed appointments and no-shows, will incur a 50% service fee.

This policy helps us protect the time reserved for you while giving other clients the opportunity to book available appointments.
